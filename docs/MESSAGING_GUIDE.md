# Messaging guide and brand copy

All copy below is what the site uses (or, where marked, a proposal). It comes from the Reset Pharmacy corporate profile and brand identity. It makes **no claim** about licences, years in business, staff, locations, awards or services that were not supplied. Anything that depends on a fact the pharmacy has not confirmed is written as an aim ("we aim to…"), not a statement of achievement.

## Voice
Warm, calm, plain. Reassuring without overpromising. Short sentences. Name what happens next. Never alarmist, never salesy about medicines, never a diagnosis.

| Say | Avoid |
|---|---|
| "A pharmacist will review it and tell you what happens next." | "Instant prescriptions", "guaranteed", "cure" |
| "We aim to…" for things not yet proven | "The best", "number one", "trusted by thousands" |
| "Ask the pharmacist" for restricted items | Persuading anyone to buy a medicine |
| "General information, not a replacement for advice from your pharmacist or doctor." | Any promise of results |
| Plain Nigerian English; naira (₦) | Jargon, hype, exclamation marks |

## Tagline
**A fresh start to feeling better.**

## Intro (home and meta description)
Everyday health essentials, clear guidance and a pharmacist who treats your questions with care.
*Meta:* Pharmacy care, wellness products and clear guidance for a fresh start to feeling better.

## About us
**A fresh perspective on everyday health.** Reset Pharmacy is a pharmacy and wellness brand built around a simple belief: access to healthcare should feel clear, reassuring, and centred on people.

**The idea behind the name.** "Reset" is the chance to take a fresh, more intentional approach to everyday health, supported by reliable information, responsible pharmacy practice, and thoughtful service.

## Vision and mission *(proposed: confirm or replace)*
- **Vision:** To become a trusted and recognisable pharmacy brand, known for responsible care, dependable service, and a more human approach to everyday health.
- **Mission:** Helping people make informed decisions about their health, by providing accessible pharmacy services, clear guidance, and a customer experience grounded in professionalism, integrity, and care.

## Values
- **Trust:** honest communication, dependable service, responsible handling of healthcare needs.
- **Professionalism:** qualified expertise, ethical conduct, respect for the standards that govern pharmacy practice.
- **People-first care:** every customer treated with dignity, patience and consideration.
- **Clarity:** product information, processes and health guidance that are easier to understand.
- **Responsibility:** safe medicine use and appropriate professional advice.
- **Continuous improvement:** practical ways to make the pharmacy experience more convenient, accessible and responsive.

## Brand promise
A more reassuring pharmacy experience: people feel listened to, information is communicated clearly, and health needs are treated with care and responsibility.

## Contact page intro
Ask about a product, a service or an order. A member of the team will reply by email. For anything urgent, please call or visit the pharmacy.

## Services and Health & Wellness intros
- **Services:** Reset Pharmacy lists only the services it has confirmed it provides. Where a service needs a pharmacist, you will always be told who does what, and what happens next.
- **Health & Wellness Centre:** Short, plain-language articles on everyday health and safe medicine use. They are general information, they cite public guidance, and they never replace advice from your pharmacist or doctor.

## Microcopy rules for medicines
1. Prescription medicines are never sold through the basket. Say so plainly.
2. A prescription upload is "a request for review, not an order".
3. Pharmacist-only items show "Ask the pharmacist", not "Out of stock" or "Sold out".
4. Health articles must carry sources and a named reviewer before they are public.
5. Never put health detail in order notes or ordinary emails; say so where customers type notes.
